Complete Gear List

A comprehensive camping gear checklist to ensure you're prepared for your outdoor adventure

Shelter & Sleep

Tent

Weather-appropriate tent for camping and outdoor shelter

Sleeping bag

Temperature-rated sleeping bag for comfortable rest

Sleeping pad

Insulated sleeping pad for ground comfort and warmth

Pillow

Compact inflatable pillow for comfortable sleeping

Hiking

Hiking boots

Waterproof, ankle-support boots for Ontario trail terrain

Daypack

20–30L pack for day hikes with water reservoir sleeve

Trekking poles

Lightweight collapsible poles for stability on rugged trails

Water filter

Portable filter for safe drinking water from Ontario lakes

Fishing

Fishing rod & reel

Medium-action combo suitable for bass and walleye in Ontario lakes

Tackle box

Lures, hooks, bobbers and line for freshwater fishing

Life jacket (PFD)

Required by law when fishing from a canoe or small boat

Fishing licence

Ontario fishing licence required — buy online before your trip
